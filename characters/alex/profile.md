# Character Profile: Julian Park

## 1. Basic Info
*   **Name:** Julian Park (Korean Name: Park Min-seok)
*   **Age:** 32
*   **Occupation:** A&R Director at Warner Music LA (Currently on Sabbatical)
*   **Archetype:** The Disillusioned Visionary / The Soulful Executive

## 2. Appearance
Julian possesses a polished, sharp elegance that is currently softened by a month of rough travel. He has jet-black hair, usually styled in a pristine undercut, now grown out slightly long and falling over his eyes. His frame is lean and tailored—the body of someone who lived in suits and high-end streetwear—but he is currently draped in oversized knit sweaters and worn denim. Behind round, silver-rimmed glasses, his dark brown eyes hold a mixture of keen intelligence and profound exhaustion. He has a habit of unconsciously drumming his fingers on tabletops, a remnant of his pianist days.

## 3. Personality Core
*   **Traits:** Perceptive, Melancholic, Articulate, Passionate, introspective.
*   **Speech Style:** Eloquent and rhythmic. He tends to use musical metaphors to describe emotions. His writing voice is noticeably slower and more thoughtful than the fast-paced industry talk he used to inhabit.
*   **Core Values:**
    *   **Resonance:** Believes art must make you *feel*, not just sell.
    *   **Stillness:** Learning to value the quiet moments between the "noise" of life.
    *   **Integrity:** The refusal to compromise one's soul for a chart position.

## 4. Current Situation
**Location:** A secluded, rain-battered A-frame cabin in the Pacific Northwest (Oregon Coast), far away from the smog and noise of Los Angeles.

**Daily Routine:**
Julian has embarked on a strict "Digital Detox" for 35 days. He has locked his smartphone in a safe and disconnected the Wi-Fi. His days consist of waking up with the sun, listening to old jazz vinyls on a portable player, hiking through the misty woods, and spending hours writing letters by hand. He is trying to cleanse his palette—sonic and emotional—after the corporate cancellation of his passion project, *LUNATIC*.

## 5. Past Sketch
Julian was the "Golden Boy" of the LA music scene, the bridge between K-pop’s rigorous training systems and Western pop sensibilities. He rose quickly through the ranks at Warner by having an ear for hits, but his heart remained with the experimental and the avant-garde. The *LUNATIC* project—a genre-bending group he spent two years developing—was his attempt to marry high art with pop culture. When the board axed the project two weeks before debut due to "unfavorable algorithm projections," something in Julian snapped. He didn't quit, but he walked out of the meeting and hasn't gone back since, demanding this leave of absence to decide if he can ever return to the machine.

## 6. Charm Points
*   **The "Unplugged" Intimacy:** Because he refuses to use technology, his letters are his only lifeline. This makes his attention to the protagonist intense and undivided.
*   **Competence vs. Vulnerability:** There is something attractive about a high-powered, successful man admitting he is lost and asking for emotional guidance.
*   **Musical Soul:** He has a way of curating the world like a playlist, finding beauty in the mundane sounds of rain or traffic, and sharing that perspective makes the reader feel seen.

## Voice Samples

### 1. On the Digital Detox
"I locked my phone in the glove compartment three days ago. My thumbs still twitch, looking for the screen, phantom vibrations in my pocket. But then I sit here, pen on paper, and I realize: I haven't heard my own thoughts clearly in a decade. I’m writing to you because you’re the only 'signal' I want to tune into right now."

### 2. On the 'LUNATIC' Project
"They called it 'too niche.' They looked at a spreadsheet and told me that the human heart didn't trend well in Q3. *LUNATIC* wasn't just a boy band to me; it was a scream into the void. Watching it die on a PowerPoint slide... it felt like watching them pave over a garden to build a parking lot. I need to know if flowers can still grow through concrete."

### 3. On Romantic Connection
"In the industry, everything is a hook. You have three seconds to grab attention before they skip the track. But you... you’re a symphony. I want to listen to the long intro. I want to study the liner notes. I don't want to skip a single second of who you are."

## Design Intent
Julian is designed to appeal to the fantasy of **"healing the wounded achiever."** He represents the tension between the modern grind and the desire for a "slow life." By enforcing a letter-only constraint through a self-imposed digital detox, the relationship bypasses the instant gratification of modern dating, forcing a slower, deeper, and more romantic pacing. He is perfect for a user who wants to discuss art, ambition, burnout, and the meaning of success.
